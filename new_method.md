# When I'm Stuck: Quick Method

## Before Starting (3-4 min)

1. **Breathe first** (1-2 min): Long exhales, normal inhales. Extend the exhale to twice the inhale (e.g., 4 sec in, 8 sec out). This activates the vagus nerve, increases HRV, and shifts the nervous system to parasympathetic. The prefrontal cortex comes back online. The DMN quiets. *Do this before trying to think your way through.*

2. **Acknowledge**: "I notice Talha feels [anxious/overwhelmed/stuck]"

3. **Validate**: "It makes sense. I learned worth = performance. Of course this shows up."

4. **Permit**: "This fear can stay. It doesn't get to decide."

5. **Reframe**: "I'm just throwing the staff. The outcome isn't mine."

6. **Two truths**: "I feel unprepared AND I can start."

7. **Good enough**: "1 Pomodoro done = success. Not good work. Just work."

8. **Start timer.**

---

## Mid-Work Surge (30 sec)

- "There it is again."
- "Makes sense."
- "You can stay. I'm still working."

---

## Before Version 2, 3...

"Version 1 didn't fail. It showed me the fear is loud today. I'm continuing, not restarting."

Then run steps 1-7 again.

---

## After Task

"I did it. I felt not-good-enough and did it anyway."

Don't evaluate. Just acknowledge survival.

---

## If Nothing Seems to Work: 5 Areas to Shift

When you're stuck and the method isn't landing, try changing one of these:

| Area | What to try |
|------|-------------|
| **Situation** | Change your environment. Move rooms. Go outside. Remove distractions. |
| **Thoughts** | Reframe the story. "I have to" → "I choose to". Challenge the catastrophe. |
| **Emotions** | Name it. Sit with it. Don't fight it. Let it pass through. |
| **Physiology** | Breathe. Cold water on face. Move your body. Eat. Drink water. |
| **Behavior** | Do the smallest possible action. One sentence. One minute. Just start. |

Sometimes the entry point isn't the mind - it's the body. Or the room. Or just moving.
