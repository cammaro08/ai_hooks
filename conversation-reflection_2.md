# Conversation Reflection - Saturday 17th January 2026

## Context

- Wife is pregnant (first trimester) with sciatic pain, on bed rest
- 1.5-year-old toddler (Hadiya)
- Head of AI Engineer, paid £75k (market rate: £120-140k)
- Financial pressures: Hadiya's citizenship, wife's visa renewal, Pakistan trip (Sept/Nov), new baby (Aug/Sept)
- Can save ~£500-890/month, needs ~£7,500 for known expenses
- £10k emergency fund (3 months worth)

---

## The Journey of the Conversation

### 1. Starting Point
Feeling sad and angry about inability to progress on goals. Every day is unpredictable - wife sick, appointments, baby care. Going to bed at 11:30pm, waking erratically (6am-9am).

### 2. First Realization - Season of Life
This isn't a planning problem, it's a survival mode season. The sadness is real but I was grieving "I can't progress on goals" when goals weren't accessible anyway with current capacity.

### 3. The Real Constraint Emerged
The goals aren't just self-improvement - they're tied to financial survival. Need a better job to cover real expenses. Job change MUST happen by July because baby comes Aug/Sept - after that it gets even harder.

### 4. Current Job IS the Prep
Doing AI strategy and leadership work. Chris Parsons (mentor) said to focus on vision, strategy, understanding the business. This builds the exact experience that gets the £100k+ job.

### 5. The Side Gig Temptation
YouTube producer role for £1000/month, 10 hours/week. Tempting for immediate cash. But realized: if I have 10 hours, job prep is the bigger lever (£25-40k/year permanent increase vs £12k/year temporary).

### 6. The Decision
No binding side gig. Can't commit to 10 hours/week with wife on bed rest. Instead:
- Work extra hours at own pace (can scale back when needed)
- 1.5 hours prep + podcasts during commute
- Set up bedtime routine with wife for consistency
- Give myself 6 months, reassess at 4-5 months
- Save £500/month baseline
- Lean on £10k emergency fund if needed, replenish later
- Either get promoted internally at mid-year, or start applying externally

### 7. Timeline Flag
Job searches take 2-4 months. To be in new job before baby (Aug/Sept), need to start applying around month 3-4, not 5-6.

---

## Key Realizations

1. **Grieving the wrong thing**: Was grieving "I can't do my goals" when I should be grieving "this is a hard season with almost no capacity"

2. **The side gig trade-off**: £1000/month immediate vs £25-40k/year permanent. The job change is the bigger lever.

3. **Current job = prep**: Building AI strategy experience IS interview prep. Document the story.

4. **Accepting "good enough"**: 1.5 hours + commute podcasts is realistic. Not 90-minute morning blocks.

5. **Emergency fund purpose**: First trimester with toddler IS an emergency. Use it, replenish later.

---

## The Final Plan

| Area | Decision |
|------|----------|
| Side gig | No - too binding, adds stress |
| Job prep | 1.5 hours/day + commute podcasts |
| Current job | Treat as prep - building AI/leadership story |
| Timeline | 6 months, reassess at 4-5, start applying by month 3-4 |
| Savings | £500/month baseline commitment |
| Emergency fund | Willing to lean on £10k if needed |
| Household | Bedtime routine with wife for consistency |
| Goal | New job by July, before baby arrives |

---

## The Core Shift

**From:** "I can't progress on anything and I feel sad and angry"

**To:** "I'll do what I can realistically do, lean on my emergency fund, build consistency with my wife, and trust that my current job is building the skills I need."

This isn't giving up. This is strategy.
